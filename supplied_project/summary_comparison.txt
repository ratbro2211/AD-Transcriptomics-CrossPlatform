“Cross-platform comparison of differentially expressed genes identified 47 genes significant in both GSE104704 (RNA-seq, Lateral Temporal Lobe, n=22) and GSE132903 (Microarray, Middle Temporal Gyrus, n=195) at padj < 0.05, |LFC| > 0.5. Strikingly, 46 of 47 overlapping genes displayed opposing directional changes between datasets (Pearson r = –0.438 across 14,962 shared genes), with genes downregulated in the LTL predominantly upregulated in the MTG and vice versa. This systematic anti-correlation reflects genuine regional transcriptomic heterogeneity in AD: the LTL and MTG differ in laminar architecture, interneuron density, and temporal vulnerability to AD pathology, explaining divergent expression of neuropeptide and synaptic genes including VGF, SST, ADCYAP1, BDNF, and RGS4. The sole concordantly downregulated gene across both datasets was ANGPTL4, suggesting it may represent a region-independent AD transcriptomic signature. Machine learning classification was consistent across platforms, with LASSO achieving the highest accuracy in both datasets (100% LOOCV for RNA-seq; 86.8% held-out test for microarray), confirming that sparse gene sets carry the AD-discriminating signal. These findings underscore both the reproducibility of core AD transcriptomic changes and the importance of brain-region specificity in interpreting differential expression results.”
